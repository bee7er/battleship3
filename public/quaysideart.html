Quayside Arts Collective

Living and working in East Sussex, Quayside Arts Collective is a group of artists who met through local art classes and discovered a shared passion for creating and exhibiting work together. Living along the coast, the members draw inspiration from the surrounding landscape, sea and everyday life. Working across a range of styles and mediums.

What started as shared studio time it has grown into a creative group of friends supporting each other’s artistic practices and working together towards regular exhibitions and creative projects.

Quayside Arts Collective provides a space for creative exchange and the opportunity to share their work with the wider community.
